You have the reputation most roofers would pay for. Your website is telling Google you work in Wimbledon and telling customers your reviews come from the Midlands.

You want more smaller, local jobs around Sutton. The good news: you already have 28 town pages and a genuinely excellent reputation. The problem is that the site is pointed at the wrong town, hides the reviews that would win the job, and cannot measure a single enquiry.

Site, Checkatrade and Google listing read 17 September 2026
Finding 1 — Your Site Tells Google You Work In Wimbledon
Your page titles — the line Google shows in search results: Home "Roofer Wimbledon | Flat Roofing Wimbledon | G L Miller Roofing" Services "Roofing Wimbledon | Roof Repairs Wimbledon | G L Miller Roofing" Gallery "Roofing Wimbledon | Pitched Roofs Wimbledon | G L Miller Roofing" Contact "Roofing Company Wimbledon | Roof Installation Wimbledon" What the site actually says: "Sutton, Surrey area" Your Google listing: 16a Vernon Road, Sutton
Every main page is aimed at a town that is not your base

The page title is the single strongest signal of where a page should rank. All four of your main pages are asking Google to show you for Wimbledon, while your address and Google listing are in Sutton and your recent Checkatrade jobs are spread across SM1, SM4, KT19, SW17 and SW20. Wimbledon may be on your patch — but your home town, where you want the local jobs, is not in a single main page title.

Finding 2 — The Testimonials Are From The Wrong End Of The Country
Testimonials on your homepage: "Sarah J., Nottingham" "James H., Derby" "Linda M., Leicester" "Rob T., Mansfield" "Emma F., Loughborough" "Claire D., Chesterfield" "Mike G., Grantham" "Hannah W., Lincoln" Your real reviews: Checkatrade 9.83 out of 10 from 102 reviews — many naming you personally Google 4.9 rating
These look like template text left in by whoever built the site

A homeowner in Cheam reading reviews from Lincoln and Grantham either thinks you are a Midlands firm or that the reviews are not real. Either way it costs you the job — and published testimonials need to be genuine customers.

You do not need them. You have over a hundred real reviews scoring 9.83, many of them naming "Gary". Those belong on the homepage, word for word, with the town each job was in.

Finding 3 — 28 Town Pages That Do Not Say Anything Local
Pages on your site: 32 Town pages: 28 — Sutton, Epsom, Banstead, Wallington × 7 each What each one has: Long general roofing text yes A photo of a job in that town no A review from someone in that town no An enquiry form no (the only form is on /contact/) A link from the main menu no (footer links only)
The hard part is done. The pages just need to prove they are local.

Most roofers have no town pages at all. You have 28. What they are missing is the thing a homeowner in Banstead actually wants to see: a job you did in Banstead, a review from someone in Banstead, and a way to ask for a quote without leaving the page. Your Checkatrade history already has the jobs — SM1, SM4, KT19, SW20.

Finding 4 — Nothing Is Being Measured
Google Analytics tag: none found (Site Kit is installed but not sending a tag) Google Ads tag: none Meta pixel: none Enquiry form on the homepage: none
You cannot grow what you cannot count

Your jobs come from referrals, Checkatrade and the website — but there is no way to tell how many come from the website, which pages bring them, or which town. The plugin to measure it is already installed; it just is not switched on.

Small Fixes While You Are Here
Each one a few minutes
  • Your email link is broken. One link is written as http://glmiller.builders@gmail.com, so tapping it opens a Gmail login page instead of an email. The other email links work.
  • Two typos on the homepage: "view our sevices" and "Quality Guarenteed".
  • A Gmail address on a firm with 102 reviews. An address on your own domain reads as more established.

What We Would Do, In Order
  • Point the site at Sutton. Rewrite the page titles for your real area — a morning's work.
  • Replace the template testimonials with your real Checkatrade and Google reviews, with the town on each.
  • Switch on measurement so every call and form is counted, by page and by town.
  • Make the town pages local — a real job, a real review and a quote form on each.
  • Then bring in the smaller local jobs you want, targeted at the postcodes you already work in and measured on jobs booked.

The reputation is already built. The website just is not showing it to the right town.

Most of this is a few days' work, not a new website.
