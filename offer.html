Why We Would Start With Advertising, And Start Now
It is the end of September

Roof repair is a weather trade. Autumn and winter storms are when ceilings start leaking and people search for a roofer the same hour they notice it. That season starts in a matter of weeks.

Somebody with water coming through a bedroom ceiling does not shop around. They search, they ring one of the first firms they see, and they book. That is the single best kind of enquiry there is, and it is the kind that advertising can put in front of you within days rather than months.

If you want more repairs fast, this is the lever. Everything else on this page is worth doing, but the rest of it compounds slowly and this does not.

The One Number We Need From You Before Any Of This
We are asking rather than assuming

You told us materials run at 80%. We want to be certain what that means, because it changes whether advertising is sensible at all.

If materials are 80% of the job price: a £400 repair leaves you £80 — and paid advertising cannot work at that level, at any price. If your margin is 80% and materials are 20%: a £400 repair leaves you £320 — and advertising works comfortably.

On a £400 repair, what is left after materials? If the answer is closer to the first line, we will tell you to take The Fix, do the free things, and keep your money. We are not going to sell you advertising that cannot pay for itself.
