Why We Would Start With Advertising, And Start Now
It is the end of September

Roof repair is a weather trade. Autumn and winter storms are when ceilings start leaking and people search for a roofer the same hour they notice it. That season starts in a matter of weeks.

Somebody with water coming through a bedroom ceiling does not shop around. They search, they ring one of the first firms they see, and they book. That is the best kind of enquiry there is, and advertising can put you in front of it within days rather than months.

The town pages and the reviews compound slowly underneath it — which is why they are in the same monthly fee rather than sold to you separately.

The One Number We Need From You Before Any Of This
We are asking rather than assuming

You told us materials run at 80%. We want to be certain what that means, because it decides whether advertising is sensible at all.

If materials are 80% of the job price: a £400 repair leaves you £80 — and paid advertising cannot work at that level, at any price. If your margin is 80% and materials are 20%: a £400 repair leaves you £320 — and advertising works comfortably.

On a £400 repair, what is left after materials? If the answer is closer to the first line, we will tell you to take The Fix, do the free things below, and keep your money. We are not going to sell you advertising that cannot pay for itself.

Two Things You Can Do This Week That We Are Not Charging For
Because they are the fastest money available to you and they are not ours to sell
  • Message your past customers before the storms. You have done hundreds of repairs and hold 102 reviews. A short note offering to check their roof before winter is the quickest work you will get this month, and it costs nothing.
  • Ask every customer for a review, every time. You are at 9.83 from 102. Your competitors are not close. That score is the reason people ring you rather than the next name on the list.

And one question worth asking yourself: you mentioned some repairs reach £2,000. Five of those is worth more than twenty call-outs at £400, on the same diesel and the same paperwork. It is worth knowing what those jobs have in common.
